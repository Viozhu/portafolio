## Direction contract

THESIS: Jorge's work is a Seoul block at 1 a.m. in the rain. Four towers on the street are four lines of work; scrolling zooms the camera into those same towers, floor by floor, then down into the station for the last train. It refuses the white hero over three cards and the static dark-mode template with one glowing accent.

OWN-WORLD: Ink #05010f to violet #1a0433 skylines, wet asphalt reflections, neon tubes in magenta #ff2a6d (applied AI), cyan #05d9e8 (product), acid yellow #f9f002 (playground, station), lilac #b9a9d6 (experience tower). Hangul vertical signs beside the floor they name, glass elevator in each facade, orange pojangmacha tents, umbrellas, a cut-away subway platform with screen doors and an amber LED board. Condensed uppercase Archivo with chromatic aberration; ui-monospace HUD with corner brackets; hangul in Gothic A1.

STORY: Visitors see the street, the name and the four towers with their roof signs; tap a sign or a tower to go in, or scroll: each floor shows the project through its window with the panel beside it, experience is the tower built since 2021, the stack lights up over the roofs, then the camera goes down the subway stairs where the last train arrives and the contact panel waits on the platform.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-viewport canvas street. HUD top (KST clock, status, signal, rain, sound, strike). Left: name in huge condensed chromatic type, role, intro, write / CV actions, hint. Right: four towers (APPLIED AI, PRODUCT, PLAYGROUND, EXPERIENCE) with neon roof signs and hangul signs hanging beside their floors. Bottom: wet street, then the newswire ticker.

FORM: Neon Rain night street plus scroll camera, user-pinned (#1, no roll). seed: none (user-pinned brief, no roll). The user pinned the world in their own words, which waived the roll: "Voy a hacer un cambio radical al diseño y me quiero basar en este prompt" (Neon Rain), then "la idea que utilice los edificios que tenemos en la pantalla principal". Signature interaction: scroll is the camera: street → tower lobby → floors (elevator readout L/1F…/R/B1/B2) → roof → station → platform; signs and towers are shortcuts. Motion grammar: neon flickers like failing glass, rain falls on one diagonal, the camera eases between stops, the train brakes in and its doors open; everything else stays still.
